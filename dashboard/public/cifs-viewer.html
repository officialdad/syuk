Citizen Incident Feed Specification for navigation system integration

What is CIFS?

The Citizen Incident Feed Specification (CIFS) is the standard data format used by Waze and Google Maps to receive road closure and incident data from external sources.


When Smart Cones are deployed at a construction site, our system automatically generates a CIFS-compatible feed containing the road closure data. This feed can be submitted to Waze's partner program, enabling real-time road hazard alerts to reach millions of drivers through their navigation apps — without any manual reporting.
